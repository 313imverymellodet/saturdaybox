About SATURDAYBOX

A tiny cable network for cartoons. Nothing is on demand: every channel follows the same broadcast clock, so whatever is on right now is on for everybody.

Arrow keys or ▲▼ change channels · type a number to jump straight to it · M toggles sound · F goes fullscreen · click any row in the guide to tune in.

On a phone, swipe across the screen to surf. Share a channel by copying the link — it remembers where you were.
